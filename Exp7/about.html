About Me

I am currently pursuing my B.E. degree in Computer Engineering, driven by a deep fascination with how software interacts directly with hardware.

My core technical interests lie in low-level programming, computer architecture, and systems design. I love tearing down complex systems to understand their inner workings, optimizing execution paths, and bridging the gap between hardware constraints and software layers. My ultimate goal is to architect software that is not only highly performant but also deeply functional and reliable.

When I am away from my code editor, you can usually find me buried in non-fiction books, exploring virtual worlds in immersive games, or breaking my brain over intricate, challenging puzzles.
